We’re here to help you get back to seeing patients.

Cosmas supports licensed clinicians and clinical-practice staff who use the app to reduce their documentation burden. § 03Security & Privacy Concerns

If you believe you have identified a security vulnerability in Cosmas, or if you suspect that PHI or other sensitive information has been exposed, please email info@cosmaneura.com with “Security” in the subject line. We will acknowledge receipt within one business day and keep you informed as we investigate.

Please do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we have had a reasonable opportunity to remediate them.

§ 04Business Associate Agreements

If you process Protected Health Information (PHI) through Cosmas, you need a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with CosmaNeura. Email info@cosmaneura.com with “BAA request” in the subject and we will send our current template for your review.

§ 05Frequently Asked Questions

I installed Cosmas but the bot isn’t joining my Zoom meetings.

The most common cause is a Zoom account permissions mismatch — for example, a calendar or Zoom integration configured at the individual-user level in a way that prevents external apps from joining on your behalf. Email dev@cosmaneura.com with the scheduled meeting ID and the email address you authorized with, and we will trace the authorization chain.

Does Cosmas work if my patients use a different video platform?

At present, Cosmas officially supports Zoom for scheduled telehealth visits. Support for Microsoft Teams and Google Meet is on our roadmap. If that is important to your practice, let us know at info@cosmaneura.com — user demand informs our build order.

Can I try Cosmas without a Business Associate Agreement?

You can evaluate the app in a non-clinical context (for example, with a simulated visit or role-play) without a BAA. As soon as you introduce Protected Health Information, a signed BAA must be in place. Email us at info@cosmaneura.com.

How do I delete my account and associated data?

Email info@cosmaneura.com from the address associated with your Cosmas account. We will confirm the request and process deletion consistent with HIPAA, applicable state record-retention rules, and your Business Associate Agreement.

Can I opt out of having my content used to improve the Cosmas AI?

Yes. We use Customer Content to improve Cosmas within the limits of our BAA with you, and we never sell or monetize it. If you would prefer to opt out of product-improvement use entirely, email info@cosmaneura.com. See our Privacy Policy, §§ 5 and 11, for details.

Where are Cosmas and CosmaNeura based?
