1. Purpose

This policy describes what data Cosma collects on behalf of customers, how that data is classified and protected, how long it is retained, and what rights users have over it. It is the operational companion to the public Privacy Policy published at cosmaneura.com.

4. Protected Health Information (PHI) handling

  • PHI that could appear in transcripts is routed through the Azure Health De-identification API before any call to a large-language-model service.
  • Access to raw (non-de-identified) recordings and transcripts is restricted to the authenticated end user who owns the data and to a minimal set of production operators acting under documented access-control procedures.
  • Cosmaneura enters into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with any sub-processor that may handle PHI on the company's behalf, consistent with HIPAA Security Rule requirements.

6. Data subject rights

Upon verified request, users may:

  • Access — receive a copy of the personal data Cosma holds about them.
  • Correct — request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Delete — request deletion of their account and associated data; deletion propagates to primary storage immediately and to backups within 30 days.
  • Export / portability — receive a machine-readable export of their transcripts and notes.
  • Withdraw consent — disconnect the Zoom integration at any time, which revokes stored OAuth tokens with Zoom.

Requests are submitted to info@cosmaneura.com and are actioned within 30 days.

7. Sub-processors & change notification

Cosma relies on the third-party processors listed in §3. A current list is maintained on the Cosmaneura website. Customers are notified by email of any material change to the sub-processor list at least 30 days before it takes effect, except where the change is required to address a security risk.

8. International transfers

Cosma's primary infrastructure is hosted in Microsoft Azure (US regions). Some sub-processors may process data outside the United States; in all such cases, the sub-processor commits to standard contractual clauses or equivalent transfer safeguards under applicable law.

9. Enforcement & review

Violations of this policy are investigated by the CTO and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. This policy is reviewed at least annually, or sooner if there is a material change in the data processed, the applicable law, or the sub-processor list.
