1. Purpose

This policy defines the security principles and minimum controls Cosmaneura applies to the Cosma application, its infrastructure, and the data it processes on behalf of customers. It exists to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer data — including Protected Health Information (PHI) — and to align the engineering team on a shared baseline of secure practices.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All production and staging environments of the Cosma application.
  • All source code, configuration, and infrastructure-as-code managed by Cosmaneura.
  • All Cosmaneura personnel (employees and contractors) with access to any of the above.
  • All third-party sub-processors that receive customer data (enumerated in the Data Protection & Retention Policy).

4. Security principles

  • Secure by default. Every new feature is designed assuming untrusted input and hostile networks; controls (authN, authZ, encryption, validation) are the default path, not an opt-in.
  • Defense in depth. No single control is relied upon; encryption, access control, monitoring, and code review each catch a different class of failure.
  • Least privilege. Personnel, services, and tokens receive the minimum permissions required for their function.
  • Privacy by design. PHI is de-identified via the Azure Health De-identification API before being sent to any third-party large-language-model service.

5. Minimum controls

AreaControl
Authentication (end users)Delegated to Clerk; MFA supported.
Authentication (personnel)SSO with MFA on GitHub, 1Password, Azure, Sentry, and Clerk admin.
Secret managementAll secrets stored in 1Password, separated by environment vault (dev / staging / production / shared). No secrets in source.
Encryption in transitTLS 1.2+ enforced for all ingress and outbound integrations.
Encryption at restAzure-managed encryption for PostgreSQL and Azure Blob Storage; application-layer AES-128 (Fernet) on sensitive columns such as Zoom OAuth tokens.
Webhook integrityHMAC-SHA256 verification of Zoom webhook challenges; Redis-based replay protection (48 h dedup window).
Source controlPrivate GitHub repository; master protected; every change requires pull-request review before merge.
CI/CD gatingAutomated lint (Ruff, ESLint), type checks (Pyright, TypeScript), migration safety (squawk-cli), and tests (pytest, Vitest) must pass before deploy.
MonitoringSentry (errors, traces) + structlog (structured JSON logs) + PostHog (product analytics); on-call rotation triages alerts.
Vulnerability managementSee separate Vulnerability Management Policy.
Incident responseSee separate Incident Response Policy.

6. Acceptable use

Personnel must not store customer data outside sanctioned systems (approved production infrastructure and approved analytics tooling). Copying production data to personal devices or to development databases is prohibited. Access to production systems requires MFA and is granted only to personnel with an active operational need.

7. Policy exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be documented, time-bounded, and approved in writing by the CTO. Exceptions are reviewed at each policy review cycle and either renewed or removed.

8. Review & revision

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or sooner when there is a material change to the architecture, the regulatory environment, or the threat model. Material revisions are versioned and recorded in the document header.
