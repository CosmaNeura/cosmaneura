Cosmas for Zoom

A step-by-step guide to installing, using, and removing the Cosmas Zoom app.

Cosmas is an AI medical scribe for Zoom telehealth visits. Once connected to your Zoom account, our bot joins the meetings you host, transcribes the conversation, and generates a structured clinical note you can review, edit, and publish. This guide explains how to add the app, use it during visits, and remove it at any time.

§ 01Overview

The Cosmas Zoom app is used by licensed clinicians and their clinical-practice staff. After you authorize Cosmas with your Zoom account, Cosmas is able to:

  • Display your scheduled and past Zoom meetings inside the Cosmas schedule view so you can pick which visits to document.
  • Send a capture bot (powered by Recall.ai) into the meetings you host so the conversation can be transcribed in real time.
  • Generate a draft SOAP-style clinical note from the transcript and send it back to you for review and publication to your EHR.
HIPAA note. If you will process Protected Health Information (PHI) through Cosmas, a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with CosmaNeura is required before you conduct real visits. Email info@cosmaneura.com with “BAA request” in the subject.

§ 02Prerequisites

  • A Cosmas account at app.cosmaneura.com. If you do not have one, email info@cosmaneura.com to request access.
  • A Zoom user account (Pro tier or above is recommended for cloud recording features).
  • Permission, under your Zoom account’s security policies, to authorize third-party Marketplace apps. Some enterprise accounts require an administrator to pre-approve the Cosmas app from the Zoom Marketplace.
  • A current browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).

§ 03Adding the app

You can add the Cosmas app to your Zoom account in two ways. Both flows end at the same authorization screen and produce the same result.

From the Zoom Marketplace

  1. Sign in to the Zoom Marketplace with the Zoom account you use for patient visits.
  2. Search for Cosmas and open the app listing.
  3. Click Add and review the requested permissions (see § 05).
  4. Click Allow. Zoom will redirect you to Cosmas to confirm the connection.
  5. When you see “Zoom connected” in your Cosmas settings, you are done.

From inside Cosmas

  1. Sign in to app.cosmaneura.com.
  2. Open Settings and locate the Zoom section.
  3. Click Connect Zoom. You will be redirected to Zoom to sign in (if needed) and authorize the app.
  4. Click Allow. Zoom will send you back to Cosmas automatically.
Only the meeting host needs to install. Co-hosts, panelists, and patients do not install anything. Cosmas operates on behalf of the clinician who authorized the app for visits that clinician hosts.

§ 04Using the app

1. Schedule your visit in Zoom

Schedule telehealth visits the way you normally do — from your Zoom client, calendar, or EHR. No special meeting settings are required. Within a few minutes of scheduling, the meeting will appear in your Cosmas Schedule view.

2. Choose which visits to document

In Cosmas, open the meeting in your Schedule and toggle “Send Cosmas bot to this visit”. You can enable it for an individual visit, or set your default to automatically join every scheduled visit (Settings → Automation).

3. Start your visit

Start the meeting in Zoom as you normally would. A few minutes before the scheduled time, the Cosmas bot (labeled Cosmas Notetaker in the participants list) will request to join. As the host, you may admit it from the waiting room. The bot does not speak, turn on video, or interact with the meeting in any way — it listens.

Patient notification. It is your professional responsibility to inform the patient that the visit is being recorded and transcribed for documentation purposes, consistent with the consent rules in your jurisdiction and with your practice’s policies.

4. End the visit

End the meeting in Zoom. The bot leaves with the meeting. Within a few minutes, a draft clinical note appears in Cosmas under Notes, linked to the patient and visit.

5. Review, edit, and publish

Open the note, review and edit it, then publish it to your EHR (or copy it out). Transcripts are retained per your retention settings and your BAA — see the Privacy Policy.

§ 05Permissions & scopes

When you authorize the Cosmas Zoom app, you grant Cosmas the following OAuth scopes against your Zoom account. Cosmas only uses them for the purposes described below.

Scope Why Cosmas requests it
user:read Read your basic Zoom profile (name, email, user ID) to link your Cosmas account to your Zoom account.
meeting:read Read scheduled and past meeting metadata (topic, start time, participants) so scheduled telehealth visits appear in Cosmas.
meeting:write Allow our bot to join meetings you host so that Cosmas can transcribe and generate clinical notes.
recording:read Access cloud recordings of meetings you host when you opt in to post-visit note generation from recorded sessions.

Cosmas never posts as you, modifies your meetings, invites other participants, changes your Zoom account settings, or accesses meetings you are not the host of.

§ 06Data handling

Cosmas processes meeting audio and transcripts to generate clinical notes. Data is transmitted over TLS, encrypted at rest, and stored on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure in the United States. Access is restricted to the clinician who authorized the app and members of their practice whom they grant access.

Full details — including retention, deletion, subprocessors, and your rights — are in the Privacy Policy.

§ 07Removing the app

You can disconnect Cosmas from Zoom at any time. There are two independent steps, and you should perform both if you want the connection fully revoked.

1. Disconnect in Cosmas

  1. Sign in to app.cosmaneura.com.
  2. Open Settings → Zoom.
  3. Click Disconnect Zoom. Cosmas will immediately delete its copy of your Zoom OAuth tokens and stop sending the bot to future meetings.

2. Uninstall from Zoom Marketplace

  1. Sign in to the Zoom Marketplace.
  2. Go to Manage → Added Apps (or use the search bar).
  3. Find the Cosmas app and click Remove.
  4. Confirm by clicking Remove in the dialog. Zoom will revoke the tokens and notify Cosmas, which immediately removes any remaining credential state.
Deleting your data. Uninstalling stops the connection going forward but does not by itself delete the notes and transcripts already produced. To request deletion of the data Cosmas has stored on your behalf, email info@cosmaneura.com from the address associated with your Cosmas account. We will process the request consistent with HIPAA, state record-retention rules, and your BAA.

§ 08Troubleshooting

The bot never joined my meeting

The most common cause is a Zoom account permissions mismatch — for example, a calendar or Zoom integration configured at the individual-user level in a way that prevents external apps from joining on your behalf. Verify that the meeting is in your Cosmas Schedule view and that “Send Cosmas bot to this visit” is enabled. If it is and the bot still did not join, email dev@cosmaneura.com with the Zoom meeting ID and the scheduled time.

I authorized Zoom but Cosmas says “not connected”

Try signing out of Cosmas and signing back in, then re-running the connect flow from Settings → Zoom. If it still fails, your Zoom administrator may have blocked third-party Marketplace apps. Ask them to pre-approve Cosmas.

The note was not generated

Note generation requires the bot to have successfully captured audio. If the host never admitted the bot from the waiting room, or if the meeting was very short, a note may not be produced. Contact dev@cosmaneura.com with the meeting ID if you believe this is in error.
