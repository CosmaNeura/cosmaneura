complete dependent change after the reviewed citation viewer/adapter source.

its identity listener before bootstrap, and discards that panel and delegation
on identity loss. Cached/rotated tokens, context, history and retained-map
responses must also match the actual SDK principal after asynchronous work.

then qualify same-record account/session replacement with a deliberately late
old response and already displayed retained content in the authorized staging
cohort. Current source/review grants and accepted historical maps remain gates.
This source-only change and its synthetic DOM tests establish no live result.

commit if necessary while preserving the receiver, viewer and immutable maps;
that reopens the prior Host gap and requires a new access decision before use.
