Tier 2 succeeds when one synthetic staging appointment produces exactly one authorized Recall capture and missed joins/dispatch failures produce a PHI-scrubbed Sentry issue plus verified human notification. Scope is #239 alerting, watchdog, dependency/ingress probes, and this plan; exclude Auth/IAM, #238, #241, deployment/OIDC/secrets, Scribe, Session Prep/Sovereign, #246, production, and outside-party contact. Use synthetic identities, meetings, media, and text only; never expose raw IDs, names, content, tokens, or secrets.
`approved source SHA -> staging-specific immutable image/digest -> staging evidence -> explicit production promotion`
Hold for the approved Auth SHA, restack normally, rerun CI, and refresh exact-head review; never force-push. Record staging/production digests built from that SHA, hold #241, and have only the release lead deploy. Prepare dedicated synthetic Clerk tenants/actors, Zoom/Recall accounts, appointment, notification route, workload/inbox read access, prior known-good staging digest, and an approved staging Sentry sink (Sentry currently initializes only in production).
Roll out receiver-first with `ZOOM_WATCHDOG_ENABLED=false`; prove every worker runs the exact SHA, old revisions are drained, and no pre-record-ID intents or legacy `app.jobs.verify_bot_joined.perform` tasks remain before enabling the producer. Never borrow production credentials or print secrets.
## Reliability and staging evidence
Each check is an encrypted record/occurrence/check-scoped inbox intent; Celery receives only its opaque ID. Uniqueness/fenced claims absorb duplicate or lost replies; bounded retries expose dead letters for authorized redrive without replaying Zoom side effects. An event ID proves local Sentry SDK acceptance only. A self-signed probe proves routing, local signer/verifier consistency, and inbox acceptance; only a provider-originated callback proves the Marketplace secret matches.
1. Freeze the approved SHA; pass focused/full tests, static checks, budget, and exact-head review. Build/deploy the staging digest and verify API, worker, scheduler, queues, Redis, database, and inbox health.
2. Authenticate through synthetic Clerk; prove persistence, correct practice, and wrong-tenant/view-as denial. Complete staging Zoom OAuth and retain sanitized proof of a provider-originated signed callback and exact appointment/provider/practice/occurrence one-use binding.
3. Admit one synthetic guest; verify exactly one bot joins, records, and leaves after native consent/chat disclosure. Speak a fixed synthetic phrase and prove one recording/transcript attaches only to the bound appointment. Replay duplicate/reordered callbacks and prove all bot, recording, transcript, binding, and lifecycle counts remain idempotent.
4. Revoke staging OAuth/access and prove a later occurrence creates no bot. Force no-join and prove the scrubbed reason-coded Sentry issue, human notification, broker-failure retry, and no stuck intent. Observe 30 minutes; retain SHA/digests, timestamps, sanitized types/counts, CI/review URLs, and workload health—never payloads.
## Abort, recovery, and outside-party gate
Abort for SHA/digest mismatch, failed CI/review, unproved provider callback, tenant/view-as bypass, missing/late disclosure, duplicates, wrong attachment, capture after revocation, absent notification, PHI telemetry, unhealthy workloads/queues, or unresolved/dead-letter intent. Stop synthetic meetings; do not delete Redis ownership keys or inbox rows. Disable the producer, wait out the 15-minute window, prove intents/queues drained, and have the release lead restore the recorded digest; preserve sanitized evidence and rerun the failed rung. Give the founder/release lead the exact SHA/digests, evidence matrix, credentials checklist, recovery owners, and meeting script covering sign-in, tenant, Zoom, disclosure, one bound visit/guest/phrase, exact attachment, departure, and health. Do not contact/schedule the outside party; pilot communication and promotion remain founder/release-lead decisions.
