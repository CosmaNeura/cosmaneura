## Review integrity and expiry

Source, plan and handoff reviews must bind their exact subject and source-bundle
hashes. Their raw requests, completed response identities, response bytes and
complete text are retained by hash. Only codex-auto-review at the fixed endpoint
is accepted. A completed response must have no error/incomplete details; output
may contain reasoning plus completed assistant text, not uncompleted messages
or tool calls. Extracted text must equal the retained text exactly.

The expected PASS and no-P0/P1/P2 decision lines must be exact, single and in
order, starting at the first text line. Quoted or fenced PASS examples, duplicate
verdicts, later conflicting findings and incomplete messages cannot clear the
gate. This format check is not independent authentication of the response.
The external independently reviewed hash anchors remain mandatory.

Review completion must follow its subject's creation, precede its applicable
expiry/order boundary and not lie in the future. Source review precedes plan;
plan review precedes handoff. Start checks preserve the existing 120-second
handoff and full 1,800-second plan remaining-lifetime floors. Retention-only
revalidation intentionally does not reopen an expired start gate or prohibit
cleanup after expiry. Wall/monotonic clocks must not diverge by over five seconds.
