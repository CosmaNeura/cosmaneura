# Staging sanitizer database-capability contract

Tier 2 on protected `master` `20bfe16dc6604530ffc973547e39c3362863ec20`; this dormant, offline-only slice defines no live role, connection, provider call, or mutation.
A future provision-job login has no membership, `SET ROLE`, ownership, DDL, or control-table write. Every database—including templates and `datallowconn=false` entries—and every direct, inherited, owner, `PUBLIC`, and effective source is authenticated. Only `staging` may have `CONNECT`; none may grant `CREATE` or `TEMP`.
The 154 public-schema types partition into 14 ACL-managed enums, 63 intrinsic row types, and 77 arrays. Enum arrays follow element usage; row arrays follow intrinsic row usability. Zero direct/effective/`PUBLIC` `USAGE` applies only to enums.
All applicable `pg_db_role_setting` rows—including database-wide `setrole=0`—plus server, startup, connection, and adapter settings are authenticated. Required values include `session_replication_role=origin`, `row_security=on`, `search_path=pg_catalog`, writable `SERIALIZABLE`, `synchronous_commit=on`, and postmaster `max_prepared_transactions=0`; exact timeouts remain blocking.

An externally fenced, server-ID-keyed lease freezes all authority writers: role/mapping/owner/ACL/default-ACL and settings changes, server configuration, and all database/schema/shared-object/migration/ad-hoc DDL. Every writer acquires before DB/provider work, retains through durable commit/rollback, validates before its own commit, and aborts on doubt. The sanitizer separately acquires before final authentication, reauthenticates all facts, retains through DB close, validates before commit, and fails closed. This mechanism remains unselected.
A separate external admission fence drains and denies every other ordinary, job, logical/background, owner/admin, provider, authority, and configuration writer. Bound to the exact server/database/UAMI/role, it admits only the sanitizer then a fresh verifier, retains through commit/rollback, both closes and controlled handoff, re-observes competitors, and fails closed. Its mechanism and enforcement authority remain unselected.
A fixed target-database advisory lock coordinates migrations and the sanitizer; it cannot fence server authority or `CREATE DATABASE`, and `ACCESS SHARE` is not exclusion.

The pinned pre-provision descriptor includes ACLs, is source-only, and cannot be reused after bootstrap changes them. Activation requires a separately generated and pinned deterministic full post-provision descriptor; selection and observation remain false. That future descriptor binds user triggers (none on INSERT for 19 rewrite tables or TRUNCATE for 62 tables) and relation structure under both held windows. Internal triggers still come from the constraint catalog, and `pg_rewrite` rules from their separate observer; revoking routine `EXECUTE` is not trigger suppression.
The future adapter owns a positive, ordered grammar derived from the sealed handoff plus exact AST/template digests; those digests remain unselected. Caller SQL/functions/identifiers and connection escape are impossible, and unlisted SELECT/CALL/DO/COPY/DDL, large-object mutation (despite PostgreSQL 16 `PUBLIC EXECUTE` creation entry points), logical messages, notifications, prepared transactions, and unlisted advisory/GUC operations are forbidden. Side-effectful dependencies are excluded. Complete `pg_largeobject_metadata` and server-wide prepared-xact observations prove zero immediately before commit and again from the fenced fresh verifier.
The pinned validator is pure and offline; all live/stability/write-path claims remain false. Activation stops at `database_role_state_authenticator_unimplemented`; recovery is a code revert.
