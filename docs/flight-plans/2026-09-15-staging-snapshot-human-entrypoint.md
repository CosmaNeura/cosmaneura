## Failure, output and preservation

Catch a capture refusal INSIDE the workspace/configuration descriptor contexts.
Their cleanup wrappers must not erase a required-human-removal error. Preserve
that specific fixed public code even if later descriptor or Terminal closure
fails. Unexpected capture exceptions conservatively require human investigation;
only fixed codes may be printed. No exception/provider/credential content leaks.

Close the held Terminal only after capture/closure and descriptor unwinding.
Local close failure after a successful capture still refuses overall acceptance.
No archive, partial encrypted configuration, metadata, receipt or claim is deleted,
and nothing is detached or replayed. Existing artifacts remain for investigation.

Success output has only the verified archive digest/size and explicit observed
human rule removal. Restore/migration acceptance and deployment authorization are
false. The entrypoint rejects missing closure, wrong digest/size or claims of
deployment/restore success. It never serializes arbitrary returned metadata.
